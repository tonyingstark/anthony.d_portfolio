About me

“I don’t just find bugs — I automate the checks, protect every release, and build what helps it grow.”

I’m a QA engineer with 8+ years of experience — from manual store testing at Piggy (acquired by Klarna) to Playwright test automation for remote teams at Theoria Medical and Project Assistant.

I write reliable end-to-end tests, run regression and cross-browser checks, and report bugs clearly so teams can ship with confidence. Alongside QA, I build GoHighLevel websites, funnels and CRM automations and edit short-form UGC video — so I understand the product side as well as the testing side.

With a background in Meta ads management and user-experience optimisation, I focus on clean, efficient work that supports real business growth.
